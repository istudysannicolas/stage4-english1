Learning Review: Part 1 📚

Verbs of Preference + -ING

Verbs expressing likes/dislikes are usually followed by a verb with -ING.

Rule: Subject + (love, like, hate, enjoy, can't stand) + Verb-ING.

Examples:
- I love playing soccer.
- She hates waking up early.

Adverbs of Frequency

They tell us how often an action happens.

Rule:
Before main verbs: I always eat.
After verb BE: I am never late.

Scale: Always (100%), Usually (90%), Often (70%), Sometimes (50%), Rarely/Hardly ever (10%), Never (0%).

Learning Review: Part 2 📚

Modal Verb CAN

Used for ability, possibility, or permission.

Rule: Use the base form of the verb after CAN/CAN'T. Do not add 's' for he/she/it.

Examples:
(+) He can swim.
(-) They cannot (can't) run fast.
(?) Can you help me? Yes, I can.

Subject & Object Pronouns

Subject Pronouns do the action (I, you, he, she, it, we, they).

Object Pronouns receive the action or follow prepositions (me, you, him, her, it, us, them).

Example:
She loves him. (Subject: She / Object: him).
